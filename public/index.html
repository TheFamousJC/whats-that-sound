DANGER — SAFETY FIRST

Don’t wait for an AI report in an emergency.

Machines: Smoke, burning smells, sparks, or a serious change in braking or steering? Stop using the machine, move to safety, and seek qualified help. Don’t run it again just to record a noise.

People & animals: Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, or immediate distress? Seek urgent medical or veterinary help. This app cannot diagnose health conditions or replace professional care.
